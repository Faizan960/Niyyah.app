# Niyyah 🕌

**Live with intention. Worship with sincerity. Return to Allah.**

Niyyah (*intention*) — formerly **SalahLock** — is a calm companion for intentional Muslim living. It gently blocks your most distracting apps during Islamic prayer windows, unblocking them once you verify that you've prayed. Around that core it offers accurate prayer times, Qibla direction, a Quran reader, a Hadith & Azkar knowledge library, streaks, and private monthly spiritual reflections.

Niyyah is not designed to capture attention — it is designed to return attention to Allah. The perfect user journey: open the app, receive guidance, leave the app, pray.

It is **not** merely a prayer app. It is **not** a productivity app. It is **not** a habit tracker. It is a peaceful, premium Islamic companion for everyday worship.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔒 Prayer Lock System
- **App blocking during prayer windows** — pick which apps get locked, with per-prayer configuration (including Jumu'ah overrides).
- **Full-screen lock overlay** appears when you open a blocked app during an active prayer window, and lifts automatically once the prayer is verified.
- **Pause SalahLock** — a temporary pause option for travel, illness, or genuine need.
- **Emergency override** — a deliberate, rate-limited escape hatch so the lock never traps you.

### ✅ Prayer Verification
A single, unified flow to gently confirm you've prayed before the lock lifts. Choose your preferred method:
- **Voice** — speech recognition with fuzzy matching, tolerant of mis-transcriptions.
- **Typing** — type a short confirmation.
- **Camera** — capture-based verification.
- Clear success and try-again states, with a configurable confirmation count.

### 🕋 Prayer Times & Qibla
- **Location-based prayer times** with offline caching, so times are always available.
- **Local Masjid mode** — enter your mosque's iqamah times instead of calculated times.
- **Qibla compass** — a luxury watch-face-inspired dial with live sensor heading.
- Reliable prayer-time alarms that survive reboots.

### 📖 Quran
- **Apple Books–style reader** — reading first, large typography, calm controls.
- Bookmarks, reading progress, surah and juz navigation, and full-text search.

### 📚 Knowledge Library
- **Hadith reader** — collections and books with search and an editorial, reading-first layout.
- **Azkar** — morning, evening, and post-prayer remembrances with counters and favorites.

### 📊 Spiritual Progress
- **Streaks & achievements** — encouraging consistency over perfection, never shaming.
- **Monthly Reflection reports** — private, generated on-device, and **strictly yours** — they never leave your phone except in your own backups.
- Daily progress ring and a prayer hero card on a calm Home dashboard.

### 🗂️ Collections & Bookmarks
- Save verses, hadith, and azkar into your own collections.
- One unified bookmarks view across Quran, Hadith, Knowledge, and Azkar — with search.

### 👤 Profile & Personalization
- **Optional Google sign-in** — bring your name and photo, or use the app fully without any account.
- **Light & dark themes** — a fully theme-aware design that stays calm and legible in both.
- Personal statistics, reading history, and monthly reflections in a journal-like profile.

### ☁️ Backup & Restore
- Full local backups of your prayer records, streaks, blocked apps, overrides, and preferences — your data stays yours.

---

## 🔐 Privacy

No analytics SDK. No Firebase. No account required. Your prayer records, streaks, and monthly reflections never leave the device except in backup files you create yourself.
